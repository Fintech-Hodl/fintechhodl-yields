How to read this

Risk score

0 to 100, lower means lower modelled risk. It combines protocol, smart-contract, asset, stablecoin, liquidity, oracle, governance and yield-durability risk. Missing data never lowers a score.

Sustainable APY

The part of the quoted APY that comes from lasting sources such as borrower interest or trading fees, as opposed to temporary token incentives.

Data sources and method

These figures are computed by FinTech Hodl, LLC from public data. We read pool, lending, price, stablecoin, protocol and exploit data from DeFiLlama, vault data from Beefy and Morpho, and cover opportunities on the Base network. Governance facts come from each protocol's public documentation. The scoring model is our own and is versioned (shown at the top of each page). Some plain-English text is written by an AI model from the structured fields and checked by a rule-based validator; each report says which. Every score is a modelled estimate and can be wrong.
